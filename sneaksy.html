                                Responsive Architecture
                            

                        


                        

                            

                                 Architecture Specs
                            

                            

                                The generated component is self-contained. The shoes are absolutely structured using left and top percentage indices inside a responsive aspect ratio parent. Toggling 3D Overflow enables layout spills utilizing absolute coordinate overrides and overflow-visible wraps.
                            

                        

                    


                
 
            
 

        
 

        
        

            
Recreation Highlights & Details

            

                

                    
                         Double-Shoe Overlap
                    
                    
In the original reference image, the banner contains two gray high-top sneakers, rotated at -42 and -32 degrees. Our recreation models these with absolute precision, letting you adjust their translation vectors in real-time, matching the shadows and depth overlap perfectly.

                

                

                    
                         3D Bound Overflow
                    
                    
The shoe on the foreground of the reference design spills out of the bottom border. By toggling 3D Overflow, we render the banner's outer clip layer visible, projecting the sneaker's leather outline beyond the boundaries with drop shadows.

                

                

                    
                         Vector Circle Grid
                    
                    
The dark charcoal background pattern is reproduced in high fidelity using responsive inline SVG rings. Adjust lines stroke weight, color, and opacity from the Styles tab, or choose from 5 sports vector presets (Waves, Stripes, Grid, Dots, Concentric Rings).
