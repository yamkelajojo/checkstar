Exercise: How to Formulate a Problem Statement
Example 1: Improving Underwriting at Infinity Life Assurance - Solution
Problem Statement:
At Infinity Life Assurance, we want to be market leaders in underwriting policies. By
increasing our underwriting by 5 times and increasing sales of 75,000 policies per
annum, we can increase revenue year on year. (Vision)
We have noticed a drop in revenue (by 14%), a below industry underwriting standard (3%
against 15%, respectively) and the miss of our target sale of 75,000 products. (Issue)
Within the data team, we will build systems to perform market analysis, predict
customised and personal policies for our clients and optimise resource allocation
towards underwriting. (Process)

Exercise: How to Formulate a Problem Statement
Example 2: Reducing faults at Viber Fibre - Solution
Problem Statement:
Viber Fiber is determined to address fault rates and provide optimal service to hold onto
the valued customers. The fibre space and business operations have identified key
revenue streams to be maximised. (Vision)
During the operational year, we have noticed an increase in technical fault reports (10%)
which is above the industry standard (5%). We also noticed a large churn rate of and
reduced customer retention. (Issue)
Optimising the fault rate detection and fixes using a data process can assist technicians
with fault reports. Data analysis and models could potentially address churn and
customer retention. (Process)

Exercise: How to Formulate a Problem Statement
Example 3: Find out what customers think of CloudBank SA - Solution
Problem Statement:
CloudBank SA has a good customer retention rate and low churn; the company is
committed to keeping its customers as their preferred banking solution. We also aspire
to reach new customers through clever marketing campaigns. (Vision)
During the recent campaign, we have lost many customers (a churn rate of 6%, up from
1%). Our marketing campaign has not seen the success we anticipated, although we
have reached more people (dropped interest in products by 10%). (Issue)
We need to investigate what our current customers are saying through better feedback
mechanisms or where we lose them through internal or external push factors. By
investigating more efficient marketing campaigns and targeting optimal clientele, we
can increase new customers. (Process)
