● Quickly respond to changes with early and continuous delivery.

● Good for a product or service that the team has not built before.
● Team roles: product owner, development team and scrum master
● Sprint time frame and dedicated sprint planning, 15min daily scrum, reviews and
retrospective. Product owner managed product backlog and task board sprint backlog.
● Visual cues and flow: kanban board, cards and swimlanes
● Small teams who need an flexible approach to delivering in a collaborative and experimental
setting... also great for personal productivity tracking
● Identify three types of waste (3M)
● Muda: activity or process that does not add value, Mura: variances in the workflow, Muri: if
there is no overload then nothing slows down.  
● A traditional, linear project management methodology. Each step is followed sequentially.
● Best suited for large projects that require strict adhesion to stages and deadlines, or projects

Scrum Format
● Run in 3 weeks sprints
● Each sprint starts with a planning session, where the
work for sprint is proposed. During these sessions
effort and business value are estimated to prioritise
team efforts
● 15 minute scrum calls each day, to discuss progress
and bring up any issues encountered (to Scrum
Master)
● Each sprint concludes with a review session, where
progress is presented to stakeholders as well as a
retrospective session which is a team-focussed
reflection on how the sprint went
