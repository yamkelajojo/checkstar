## 12. Recommendations

### 12.1 Brand Recommendations

1. **Modernise visual identity**: The header image dates from April 2016. A refreshed logo and visual system would signal current relevance.
2. **Consistent social presence**: Instagram and Facebook profiles appear under-maintained. Active content would strengthen community brand positioning.
3. **Clarify the "Checkstar" vs "Check Star" naming**: Used interchangeably across the site and third-party platforms — standardisation recommended.

### 12.2 UI/UX Recommendations

1. **Mobile-responsive redesign is critical**: The fixed 990px table layout is functionally broken on mobile devices.
2. **Implement semantic HTML**: Proper heading hierarchy, alt text on images, and ARIA labels for accessibility.
3. **Add site search and product browsing**: The current products page is a text-only list with no images, prices, or details.
4. **Integrate the "Now Now" app experience**: Bridge the gap between the brochure website and the delivery app.

### 12.3 Engineering Recommendations

1. **Migrate from static XHTML**: Move to a modern stack (React/Next.js, Vue/Nuxt, or at minimum a CMS like WordPress).
2. **Enable HTTPS**: The site currently serves over unencrypted HTTP.
3. **Implement structured data**: Product, organization, and local business schema for SEO.
4. **Add proper analytics**: Google Analytics or similar beyond Facebook Pixel alone.
5. **Create robots.txt and sitemap.xml**: Essential for search engine discoverability.

### 12.4 Content Recommendations

1. **Product catalogue online**: Images, prices, and stock information would drive engagement.
2. **Current specials/catalogues**: Rather than relying on third-party catalogue aggregators, host specials directly.
3. **Store expansion information**: If the ambition is "every city in South Africa," communicate progress.
4. **Update community content**: Most CSR/gallery content dates from 2014–2015.
