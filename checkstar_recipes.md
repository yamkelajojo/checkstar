# Coconut Burfi

**Ingredients:**
- 500g Klim Milk Powder
- 125ml Nestle Dessert Cream
- 125ml Melted Ghee
- 250g Sugar
- 125ml Water
- 125g Ground Almonds
- 125g Thick Ghee
- 500ml Desiccated Coconut
- 125ml Nestle Dessert Cream - Extra
- 3g Cardamom Powder (Elachi)
- 1 Tin Nestle Condensed Milk
- Tinted Almond Slices For Decoration

**Method:**
Place 500g Klim, 125ml dessert cream, and 125ml melted ghee in a mixing bowl, rub until evenly mixed & set aside. Combine sugar & water in a sauce pan, allow to dissolve, bring to a boil for 10 minutes or to a soft ball stage. Place a drop of syrup into a glass of water and it will form a soft ball when rolled between your fingers. Add ground almonds, thick ghee, coconut, extra dessert cream, cardamom and kihn mixture. Cook over low heat, whilst stirring until the mixture appears dryish, add Nestle condensed milk, continue to stir over gentle heat until mixture has thickened (about 30 minutes). Decorate with almonds allow to cool and cut into squares or triangles (allow 8 hours setting time before serving).

# Date Roll In Coconut

**Ingredients:**
- 125g Butter Or Margarine Softened
- 80g Sugar
- 2 x Extra Large Eggs
- 5ml Vanilla Essence
- 500g Cake Flour
- 5g Baking Powder
- 1g Salt
- 125g Pitted Dates

**Coating:**
- 200ml Milk
- 80g Desiccated Coconut

**Method:**
Cream butter & sugar together, add one egg at a time, beating well after each addition until light and creamy, add essence. Sift flour, baking powder & salt together, add to egg mixture and knead to a soft dough. Take a small piece of dough & press a piece of date in to the centre. Roll each into an oval, long finger shape. Complete with rest of dough and dates.

**Coating:**
Dip date rolls into milk & then coat with coconut & place on greased baking trays. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C for 20 - 25 minutes. Keep in an airtight container for about 1 week.

# Gulab Jamun

**Ingredients:**
- 750g Cake Flour
- 250g Semolina
- 5g Baking Powder
- 3g Ground Cardamom (Optional)
- 5g Ground Nutmeg
- 125g Thick Ghee
- 1 Tin Condensed Milk
- 3ml Vanilla Essence
- 100ml Water
- 500ml Cooking Oil
- Desiccated Coconut

**Syrup:**
- 625ml Sugar
- 300ml Water

**Method:**
Sift flour, semolina, baking powder cardamom & nutmeg. Add ghee, condensed milk, essence, add enough water to make dough soft. Leave for 30 minutes. Shape into long finger size rolls. Place on a tray deep fry in hot oil.

**Syrup:**
Boil sugar, water and keep warm over low heat. Deep fry rolls in hot oil, drain & dip immediately in hot syrup. Roll lightly in coconut.

**Variation:**
Substitute the vanilla essence with almond / lemon / elachi or any essence of choice.

# Lamington

**Ingredients:**
**Cake:**
- 3 Large Eggs
- 2/3 Cup Castor Sugar
- 1 Teaspoon Vanilla Essence
- 1 Tablespoon Oil or Melted Butter
- 3 Tablespoon Milk
- 1 Cup Cake Flour
- 3 Teaspoon Baking Powder
- Pinch Of Salt

**Coating:**
- 1¾ Cups Of Water
- 2½ Tablespoon Cocoa
- 60g Butter Or Margarine
- ½ Teaspoon Vanilla Essence
- 1¾ Cups Of Icing Sugar

**Method:**

**Cake:**
Sift dry ingredients in separate bowl and place aside. Beat together eggs & castor sugar till light and fluffy. Place melted butter, water & milk in a sauce pan, bring to boil. Add & lightly fold dry ingredients into egg mixture with a wooden spoon. Lastly add boiled liquid (still hot) and stir vigorously till smooth. Pour mixture into greased tray and bake for approximately 10 minutes. Turn out onto cooking rack & cool completely.

**Coating:**
Place all ingredients in a sauce pan, bring to boil stir until mixture thickens slightly, remove from direct heat, keep mixture warm all the time. Using 2 forks, dip the squares into the coating. Finally roll in desiccated coconut.

**Frosting:**
In a mixing bowl beat the butter with a spoon until smooth, then start to sift in the icing sugar, stirring regularly. Add the vanilla extract and milk. The frosting should be light and creamy, add food colouring into the icing to give an even colour.

# Hungarian Tart

**Ingredients:**
- 125g Butter
- 50ml Sour Milk
- 2 Table Spoon Cooking Oil
- 2 Table Spoon Vanilla Essence
- 4 Teaspoon Baking Powder
- 2 Table Spoon Castor Sugar
- 2 Cups Flour
- Jam to spread & Icing Sugar To Sprinkle Over The Top Of Tart

**Method:**
Cream together butter & sugar. Stir in cooking oil, sour milk & vanilla essence. Add in the dry ingredients & mix well to form dough. Place & flatten 3/4 of the dough onto a greased tray. Spread a generous amount of jam over the dough grate the remainder of the dough over the jam & bake at 180 degrees for approximately 45 minutes. When completely cooled, sift icing sugar over the tart.

# Carrot Cake
*For the Easter bunny :)*

**Cake Ingredients:**
- 4 eggs
- 1 1/4 cups vegetable oil
- 2 cups white sugar
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 3 cups grated carrots
- 1 cup chopped pecans nuts

**Icing Ingredients:**
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1 cup cream cheese, softened
- 4 cups icing sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup chopped pecans nuts

**Method:**

**For the Cupcakes:**
1. Preheat oven to 175°C. Grease and flour a 9x13 inch pan.
2. In a large bowl, beat together eggs, oil, white sugar and 2 teaspoons vanilla. Mix in flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt and cinnamon. Stir in carrots. Fold in pecans.
3. Bake in the preheated oven for 40 to 50 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the cake comes out clean. Let cool in pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack and cool completely.

**For the icing:**
1. In a medium bowl, combine butter, cream cheese, icing sugar and 1 teaspoon vanilla.
2. Beat until the mixture is smooth and creamy. Stir in chopped pecans. Spread the cooled cake.

# Easter Cupcakes

**Cupcake Ingredients:**
- 115g margarine
- 115g caster sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 115g self-raising flour
- 1 tablespoon cocoa
- ½ teaspoon baking powder

**Icing Ingredients:**
- 85g margarine
- 1-2 tablespoons milk
- 1 heaped tablespoon cocoa
- 225g icing sugar, sieved
- 100g mini chocolate eggs, to decorate

**Method:**

**For the Cupcakes:**
1. Pre-heat your oven to 190°C.
2. Sieve the flour, cocoa and baking powder. Add the remaining ingredients into a mixing bowl and beat with a wooden spoon for 2 - 3 minutes until well combined.
3. Spoon the mixture into 18 - 20 paper cupcake cups.
4. Place them on the second from top shelf of your pre-heated oven and bake for 15 - 20 minutes.
5. Take your cupcakes out and leave them to cool.

**For the icing:**
1. Beat all your icing ingredients together in a mixing bowl until smooth.
2. Spread or pipe the mixture over each cake to form a little nest, and pop a few chocolate eggs on top.
